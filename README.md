# AI Lead Qualification Agent

## Overview
The AI Lead Qualification Agent helps businesses evaluate potential customers and identify leads that are most likely to convert into clients.

It analyzes information such as customer requirements, budget, urgency, and project timeline to classify leads as **HOT, WARM, or COLD**.

## Features
- Analyzes potential customer information.
- Evaluates budget and project requirements.
- Classifies leads as HOT, WARM, or COLD.
- Helps prioritize sales opportunities.
- Reduces manual lead evaluation.

## How It Works
1. Receive lead information.
2. Analyze the customer's needs, budget, and urgency.
3. Evaluate the lead based on defined criteria.
4. Assign a lead category.
5. Return the qualification result.

- Lead data processing

## Use Cases
- Sales teams
- Small businesses
- Marketing agencies

## Project Goal
To automate lead qualification, save time, and help businesses focus on the most promising potential customers.
